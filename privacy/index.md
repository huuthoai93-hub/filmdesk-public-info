# FilmDesk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

FilmDesk ("the app") is a film photography logbook for Android, made by an individual developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive. The developer does not run any server and does not collect your data.**

## 1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- Your logbook entries: film rolls, film stock, cameras and lenses, labs, notes, dates and captions you enter.
- Copies of photos you choose to add to a roll, if you pick a copy mode that keeps a copy in the app.
- References (links) to the original photos in your device's gallery.
- Your settings, such as language, theme, notification preferences and the email address of the Google account you connected for Drive backup.

This data is not sent to the developer. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from your device.

Android's own Auto Backup may back up the app's database and settings to your Google account, as it does for most apps. Photo copies are excluded from Auto Backup. You can turn Auto Backup off in your device's settings.

## 2. Photos permission

The app asks for permission to read photos on your device (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, or "selected photos only" if you choose that). It uses this permission only to:

- show the photos you added to your logbook,
- check whether the original photo still exists on your device, so it can warn you before you remove a photo and choose the right source for Drive backup.

The app does not scan, analyse, upload or share photos you have not added to a roll.

## 3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Drive backup, the app signs in with your Google account and uploads the photos you added to your logbook to **your own Google Drive**, in a folder named "FilmDesk".

- The app requests only the `drive.file` scope. It can see and manage only the files it created itself. It cannot read any other files in your Drive.
- The app only adds files to Drive. It never deletes anything from your Drive. Removing a photo or a roll in the app does not remove it from Drive.
- The app can download its own backup files from your Drive to restore missing photo copies.
- Access tokens are not stored by the app. The developer never receives your files, your Google account password or your tokens.
- You can disconnect Drive in Settings, and remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 4. Purchases (FilmDesk Pro)

FilmDesk Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. The developer does not see or store your payment details. The app stores only whether Pro is unlocked, on your device, and checks this with Google Play. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local notifications, such as Drive backup progress and film expiry reminders. They are created on your device and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in Android settings.

## 6. What the app does not do

- No advertising and no advertising ID.
- No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
- No account with the developer and no developer-run server.
- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
